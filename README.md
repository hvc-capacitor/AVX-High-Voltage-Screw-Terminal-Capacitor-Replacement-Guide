# AVX-High-Voltage-Screw-Terminal-Capacitor-Replacement-Guide

**AVX HP / HD Screw Terminal Capacitor Replacement Guide**

AVX's HP and HD screw-terminal high voltage ceramic capacitor series are end-of-life. This guide lists AVX HP/HD part numbers and their HVC drop-in replacements.

📖 **Full guide with the complete cross-reference table: https://hvc-capacitor.github.io/avx-screw-terminal-capacitor-alternative/**

## Series covered

- AVX HP series — screw terminal, end-of-life
- AVX HD series — screw terminal, end-of-life
- Y5U and N4700 dielectric variants covered
